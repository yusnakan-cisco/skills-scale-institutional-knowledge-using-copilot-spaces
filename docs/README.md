# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central entry point to OctoAcme’s project management guidance. These documents describe how teams initiate, plan, deliver, release, and continuously improve projects.

## Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme moves work through initiation, planning, execution and tracking, release, and retrospective improvement. Teams validate the business need and measurable outcomes, align stakeholders, and document initial risks and resource needs before moving into planning. Approved work becomes a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria, owners, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and plans for risks and dependencies.

During execution, teams coordinate through project boards, standups, delivery syncs, and sprint or milestone demos. The Project Manager (PM) coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; the Product Manager (PdM) defines outcomes, priorities, and success measures; developers build and review changes; QA/testing validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks are reviewed regularly, updates use a shared source of truth, and blockers escalate from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed.

Quality is built into delivery with small reviewed pull requests, automated tests and linting in CI, security scanning, and appropriate unit, integration, end-to-end, or manual testing. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing checks, release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback or mitigation plan; teams verify deployments and communicate outcomes to stakeholders. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture learning as owned, time-bound improvement actions.
